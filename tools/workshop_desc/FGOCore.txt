A shared mechanics library for the Fate/Grand Order Servant character mods, built on the BaseLib framework.

About this mod:
- Provides the core systems every FGO Servant shares: the NP / Overcharge gauge, Form (stance) switching, Bulwark block-retention, Bond affinity bonuses, and Critical Stars.
- This is a LIBRARY: it adds no playable character on its own. It is REQUIRED by the FGO Servant mods (Mash Kyrielight, Morgan, Artoria Caster, and more).
- Also requires BaseLib - subscribe to it as well.

Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.

Made with the help of Claude Code.
