The shared mechanics library that powers the Fate/Grand Order Servant character mods. Built on the BaseLib framework, it gives every FGO Servant a common foundation so they feel like they belong to the same roster.

What it provides:
- NP / Overcharge gauge: a charge meter (0-300) that builds as you fight and unleashes each Servant's Noble Phantasm and overcharge effects.
- Form switching: stance/aspect changes mid-combat (e.g. Berserker to Caster), each form rewriting how the Servant plays.
- Bulwark: block that is retained between turns instead of vanishing.
- Bond (affinity): relics that lift a Servant's damage, block and regen the more you play them.
- Critical Stars and Curses: shared FGO resources used across the cast.
- A handful of colorless "FGO meme" cards.

This is a LIBRARY - it adds no playable character on its own, and is REQUIRED by every FGO Servant mod (Mash Kyrielight, Morgan, Artoria Caster, and more). Also requires BaseLib.

Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.

这是一个为「Fate/Grand Order」从者角色 Mod 提供底层机制的共享模组库，基于 BaseLib 框架打造。它给每位 FGO 从者打下统一的机制地基，让大家用起来像出自同一套班底。

本库提供的核心机制：
- 宝具 / 充能槽（Overcharge）：战斗中不断累积的能量槽（0-300），用来释放各从者的宝具与超充能效果。
- 形态切换：战斗中切换姿态/阵位（例如狂战士切换到术士），每个形态都会改写从者的打法。
- 壁垒（Bulwark）：跨回合保留、不会回合结束清零的格挡。
- 羁绊（好感度）：随着你不断游玩同一从者，逐步提升其伤害、格挡与每回合回血的圣遗物。
- 暴击星与诅咒：全体从者通用的 FGO 资源。
- 一小批无色的「FGO 梗」卡牌。

注意：这是一个「机制库」，本身不含任何可操作角色，是所有 FGO 从者 Mod（玛修·基列莱特、摩根、阿尔托莉雅·卡斯特等）的必装前置。另需 BaseLib。

依赖：仅需 BaseLib。

支持语言：英文、西班牙文、简体中文。
