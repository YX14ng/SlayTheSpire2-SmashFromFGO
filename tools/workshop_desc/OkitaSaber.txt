Okita Souji (Saber) of Fate/Grand Order, as a fully playable character. Built on the BaseLib framework and the FGO Core library.

What it adds:
- A Quick crit single-target glass cannon whose real limit is the Breath of a dying body: Bursts cost both energy and Breath, and running out gives you a Cough status card.
- Attacking is the engine: play attacks to bank Critical Stars, land crits to charge NP and refund Breath - then decide whether to spend the air now or survive another turn.
- Her Noble Phantasm, Mumyou Sandanzuki - three simultaneous thrusts that pierce Block, and become five with a full breath.

Requires: FGO Core (library) + BaseLib.
Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.

「Fate/Grand Order」的冲田总司（剑士），现已作为可操作角色登场。基于 BaseLib 框架与 FGO Core 库制作。

本 Mod 带来：
- 一名 Quick 暴击、单体爆发的脆皮炮台，真正的瓶颈是这具病躯的「气息」(Breath)：强力爆发既要耗能量也要耗气息，气息耗尽就会塞给你一张「咳嗽」状态卡。
- 攻击就是引擎：打出攻击攒暴击星，打出暴击充宝具并回复气息——然后再决定是当下把这口气全用掉，还是留着多撑一回合。
- 宝具「Mumyou Sandanzuki」（无明·三段突）——三段同时突刺、穿透格挡；气息全满时更会化作五段。

依赖：FGO Core（库）+ BaseLib。
支持语言：英文、西班牙文、简体中文。
