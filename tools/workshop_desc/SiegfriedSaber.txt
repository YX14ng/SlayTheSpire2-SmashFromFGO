[h1]Siegfried — Armor Forged from Dragon Blood[/h1]
Reduce every incoming hit with persistent Dragon Scales, survive what should be lethal and choose when to sacrifice the armor for a decisive eruption.

[b]RELEASE BUILD — v0.1.12[/b]

[h2]Core gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dragon Scales:[/b] persistent reduction applies after Block. It is not Block, is not consumed and remains between turns.
[*][b]The Linden Leaf:[/b] the first attack that reaches you each turn ignores only 1 Scale; remaining stacks still reduce it, and later hits use the full value.
[*][b]Scale Eruption:[/b] sacrifice the entire defensive engine to damage every enemy. The safest position can become the strongest offensive turn.
[*][b]Balmung:[/b] charge the Noble Phantasm and let its payoff scale with the dragon-blood defense built during the fight.
[/list]

[*]Animated official FGO battle model with a grounded attack sequence
[*]Curated official artwork for cards, powers, relics and character UI
[*]Spanish, English, Simplified Chinese, Korean and Russian localization
[/list]

[h2]Mecánicas principales[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Escamas de Dragón:[/b] una reducción persistente se aplica después del Bloqueo. No es Bloqueo, no se consume y permanece entre turnos.
[*][b]Hoja de Tilo:[/b] el primer ataque que te alcanza cada turno ignora sólo 1 Escama; las restantes todavía lo reducen y los golpes siguientes usan el valor completo.
[*][b]Erupción de Escamas:[/b] sacrificá todo el motor defensivo para dañar a cada enemigo. La posición más segura puede transformarse en el turno más ofensivo.
[*][b]Balmung:[/b] cargá el Noble Phantasm y hacé que su resultado escale con la defensa de sangre de dragón construida durante el combate.
[/list]

[h1]简体中文[/h1]
用持续生效的龙血鳞片削减每一段伤害、挺过本应致命的攻击，并选择何时牺牲铠甲发动决定性的爆发。

[b]公开版本 — v0.1.12[/b]

[h2]核心机制[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]龙血鳞片：[/b]持续减伤会在格挡后生效。它不是格挡，不会被消耗，并在回合间保留。
[*][b]菩提叶：[/b]每回合第一次击中你的攻击仅无视1点鳞片；其余层数仍会减伤，之后的攻击则使用完整数值。
[*][b]鳞甲爆发：[/b]牺牲全部防御体系，对所有敌人造成伤害。最安全的局面也能转化为最强的进攻回合。
[*][b]Balmung：[/b]充能宝具，让高潮效果随战斗中建立的龙血防御一同成长。
[/list]

[h2]内容[/h2]
[list]
[*]41 个卡牌模型，涵盖初始牌组、奖励、宝具及衍生卡
[*]8 个角色能力与 8 件专属遗物
[*]FGO 官方战斗模型动画，采用贴近地面的短攻击序列
[*]卡牌、能力、遗物与角色界面均采用精心筛选的官方美术
[*]支持西班牙语、英语、简体中文、韩语与俄语
[/list]
