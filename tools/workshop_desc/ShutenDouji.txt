[h1]Shuten-Douji — Distill Poison into Sake[/h1]
Weave Assassin and Caster cards through one deck, turn Poison into a 0–100 Sake economy and choose which of two Noble Phantasms will end the banquet.

[b]PLAYTEST BUILD — v0.1.1[/b]
The character and presentation are complete for testing; balance, save/load and co-op validation are still in progress.

[h2]Core gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Sake:[/b] a personal resource that remains between turns. Assassin produces it through Poison and control; Caster spends it to improve damage and defense.
[*][b]Cross:[/b] play an Assassin and a Caster card in the same turn to unlock hybrid effects. These are card styles inside one deck, not forms that force a transformation.
[*][b]Command Cards:[/b] Buster supplies impact, Arts charges NP and Quick generates Critical Stars through FGO Core's shared systems.
[*][b]Two Noble Phantasms:[/b] reaching 100 NP manifests both choices. Select an area Assassin attack with Poison and Skill Seal, or a six-hit Caster burst empowered by Sake.
[/list]

[h2]Content[/h2]
[list]
[*]68-card reward pool and a 10-card starting deck
[*]2 mutually exclusive manifested Noble Phantasms
[*]37 character powers and 12 relics, including 3 starting relics
[*]Animated official Assassin battle model: idle, Quick attack, cast, hurt and death
[*]Official artwork selected individually for cards, powers, relics and character UI
[*]Spanish, English and Simplified Chinese text; Korean and Russian files use a safe English fallback
[/list]

[list]

[*][b]Cruce:[/b] jugá una carta Assassin y una Caster en el mismo turno para activar efectos híbridos. Son estilos dentro de un único mazo, no formas que obligan a transformarse.

[h1]简体中文[/h1]
在同一套牌组中交织 Assassin 与 Caster 卡牌，把毒素酿成 0–100 的酒资源，并从两种宝具中选择宴席的终幕。

[b]测试版本 — v0.1.1[/b]
角色与视觉内容已可完整测试；平衡、存档及联机验证仍在进行。

[h2]核心机制[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]酒：[/b]可在回合间保留的专属资源。Assassin 通过毒与控制生成酒；Caster 消耗酒来强化伤害与防御。
[*][b]交错：[/b]同一回合打出 Assassin 与 Caster 卡牌即可启动混合效果。二者是同一牌组中的卡牌风格，并非强制变身的形态。
[*][b]指令卡：[/b]Buster 提供爆发，Arts 充能 NP，Quick 则通过 FGO Core 共用系统生成暴击星。
[*][b]双宝具：[/b]达到 100 NP 时会同时显现两个选择。可选附带毒与技能封印的 Assassin 群体攻击，或由酒强化的 Caster 六连击。
[/list]

[h2]内容[/h2]
