[h1]Morgan — Queen, Witch and Winter Sovereign[/h1]
Play Fate/Grand Order's Fairy Queen as a fully animated character who curses the battlefield, dances between two lives and chooses which Noble Phantasm will answer when the gauge fills.

[b]RELEASE BUILD — v0.1.16[/b]

[h2]Core gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Curse and judgement:[/b] seed enemies with persistent Curse, then turn those marks into stronger attacks, executions and battlefield control.
[*][b]Queen and Rain Witch:[/b] Morgan's Berserker form attacks and plants Curse; Aesc charges NP and survives through defense. Crossing 100 NP manifests the Noble Phantasm of the active form.
[*][b]Winter coronation:[/b] a rare permanent transformation combines both identities and ends the form dance with the strongest version of the Queen.
[*][b]Buster and Criticals:[/b] aggressive Command Cards build pressure while Quick attacks feed the shared Critical Star system for ×1.5 finishing turns.
[/list]

[h2]Content[/h2]
[list]
[*]81 card models across the starting deck, rewards, two Noble Phantasms and generated cards
[*]19 character powers and 10 relics
[*]Animated Fairy Queen, Rain Witch and Winter Queen forms
[*]Curated official artwork for cards, powers, relics and character UI
[*]Spanish, English, Simplified Chinese, Korean and Russian localization
[/list]

[*][b]Reina y Bruja de la Lluvia:[/b] Morgan Berserker ataca y maldice; Aesc carga NP y resiste con defensa. Cruzar 100 NP manifiesta el Noble Phantasm de la forma activa.

[h1]简体中文[/h1]
扮演《Fate/Grand Order》的妖精女王：诅咒战场、在两段人生间切换，并决定 NP 充满时由哪一种宝具回应。

[b]公开版本 — v0.1.16[/b]

[h2]核心机制[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]诅咒与判决：[/b]为敌人施加持续诅咒，再把标记转化为更强攻击、处决与战场控制。
[*][b]女王与雨之魔女：[/b]狂战士摩根负责攻击与施咒；梣负责充能 NP 与防守。突破 100 NP 时会显现当前形态的宝具。
[*][b]冬之加冕：[/b]稀有的永久变身会结合两种身份，以女王的最终姿态结束形态循环。
[*][b]Buster 与暴击：[/b]进攻型指令卡持续施压，Quick 攻击则为共用暴击星系统供能，创造 ×1.5 的终结回合。
[/list]
