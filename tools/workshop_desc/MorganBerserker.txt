Morgan, the Fairy Queen of Fate/Grand Order, as a playable character. Built on the BaseLib framework and the FGO Core library.

About this mod:
- Adds Morgan as a fully playable, fully animated character.
- A Buster-crit and Curse playstyle: stack critical power and bury your enemies in Curses.
- Switch forms mid-combat between her Berserker and Caster aspects.
- Her Noble Phantasm, Roadless Camelot: the ideal land that can no longer be reached.

Requires: FGO Core (library) + BaseLib.
Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.

Made with the help of Claude Code.
