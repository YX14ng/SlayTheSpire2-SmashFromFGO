Morgan, the Fairy Queen of Fate/Grand Order - the tyrant who built Britain on sixty years of vengeance - as a fully playable, fully animated character. Built on the BaseLib framework and the FGO Core library.

What it adds:
- A complete character with her own card pool and starting relic.
- A Buster-crit and Curse playstyle: stack critical power to land devastating attacks, while burying your enemies under stacks of Curse that bleed them every turn.
- Form switching: shift mid-combat between her Berserker and Caster (Winter Queen) aspects, each one reshaping her deck and her authority.
- Her Noble Phantasm, Roadless Camelot - "the ideal land that can no longer be reached."
- Royal relics and curse-payoff cards that reward a patient, escalating game.

Requires: FGO Core (library) + BaseLib.
Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.

「Fate/Grand Order」的妖精女王摩根——那位用六十年复仇之心铸成不列颠的暴君，现已作为可操作、全动画的角色登场。基于 BaseLib 框架与 FGO Core 库制作。

本 Mod 带来：
- 一名完整角色，配有专属卡池与初始圣遗物。
- Buster 暴击 + 诅咒的玩法：堆叠暴击威力打出毁灭性的攻击，同时把敌人埋进层层「诅咒」之下，让它们每回合持续掉血。
- 形态切换：战斗中在狂战士与术士（冬之女王）两个阵位之间来回切换，每个形态都会重塑她的牌组与权能。
- 宝具「Roadless Camelot」——「那片再也无法抵达的理想之地」。
- 女王专属圣遗物与诅咒结算卡，奖励耐心铺垫、滚雪球式越打越强的打法。

依赖：FGO Core（库）+ BaseLib。
支持语言：英文、西班牙文、简体中文。
