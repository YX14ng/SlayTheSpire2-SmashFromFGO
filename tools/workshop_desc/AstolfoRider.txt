[h1]Astolfo — Follow the Next Caprice[/h1]
Match a different Command each turn, turn Quick attacks into Critical Stars and summon Hippogriff to escape the hits that truly matter.

[b]PLAYTEST BUILD — v0.1.9[/b]
The character and presentation are complete for testing; balance, save/load and co-op validation are still in progress.

[h2]Core gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Caprice bag:[/b] a visible no-repeat bag draws Quick, Arts or Buster. Match the Command to gain Stars, NP Charge or bonus damage—or manipulate the bag when the hand has better plans.
[*][b]Critical Stars:[/b] Quick attacks generate Stars through FGO Core. Save 50 to prepare a ×1.5 Critical, or spend Stars on Energy, NP and Evasion.
[*][b]Evasion:[/b] up to 3 charges avoid enemy Attack hits after Block and Buffer. Cards can preserve, spend or convert those charges into defense.
[*][b]Hippogriff:[/b] a Sure Hit, unblockable area Quick Noble Phantasm that consumes all NP, fills Evasion and generates Overcharge Stars.
[*][b]Four treasures:[/b] Argalia's lance, Casseur de Logistille, La Black Luna and Hippogriff provide control, cleansing and boss answers without becoming a random generated arsenal.
[/list]

[*]35 character powers and 12 relics

[*]Official artwork selected for cards, powers, relics and character UI

[h2]Mecánicas principales[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Bolsa de Caprichos:[/b] una bolsa visible sin repeticiones elige Quick, Arts o Buster. Cumplí la Orden para ganar Estrellas, Carga NP o daño adicional, o manipulá la bolsa cuando la mano tenga otros planes.
[*][b]Estrellas de Crítico:[/b] los Ataques Quick generan Estrellas mediante FGO Core. Guardá 50 para preparar un Crítico de ×1,5, o gastalas en Energía, NP y Evasión.
[*][b]Evasión:[/b] hasta 3 cargas evitan impactos de Ataques enemigos después de Bloqueo y Buffer. Las cartas pueden conservarlas, gastarlas o convertirlas en defensa.
[*][b]Hippogriff:[/b] un Noble Phantasm Quick de área, Certero e imbloqueable que consume todo el NP, completa Evasión y genera Estrellas por Sobrecarga.
[*][b]Cuatro tesoros:[/b] la lanza de Argalia, Casseur de Logistille, La Black Luna e Hipogrifo aportan control, limpieza y respuestas contra jefes sin volverse un arsenal aleatorio generado.

[h1]简体中文[/h1]
每回合遵循不同指令、把 Quick 攻击转化为暴击星，并召唤骏鹰避开真正致命的攻击。

[b]测试版本 — v0.1.9[/b]
角色与视觉内容已可完整测试；平衡、存档及联机验证仍在进行。

[h2]核心机制[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]任性袋：[/b]可见且不重复的袋子会抽取 Quick、Arts 或 Buster。匹配指令可获得暴击星、NP 充能或额外伤害；手牌另有计划时也能操纵袋子。
[*][b]暴击星：[/b]Quick 攻击通过 FGO Core 生成暴击星。保留 50 星可准备 ×1.5 暴击，也可将星用于能量、NP 与闪避。
[*][b]闪避：[/b]最多 3 层，在格挡与缓冲之后避开敌方攻击段数。卡牌可以保留、消耗闪避，或将其转化为防御。
[*][b]骏鹰：[/b]具有必中且无法被格挡的 Quick 群体宝具。它会消耗全部 NP、补满闪避，并通过过充能生成暴击星。
[*][b]四件宝具：[/b]阿尔加利亚之枪、破却宣言、恐慌呼唤与骏鹰提供控制、净化及对 Boss 手段，而非随机生成的武器库。
[/list]
