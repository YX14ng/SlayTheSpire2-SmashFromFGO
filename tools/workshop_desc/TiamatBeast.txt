[h1]Tiamat — Raise the Swarm, Awaken the Beast[/h1]
Seed Curse as Tiamat-Larva, nurture the Lahmu and unleash a second combat deck when the Beast of Regression awakens.

[b]RELEASE BUILD — v0.1.20[/b]

[h2]Core gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Two decks:[/b] Lily controls the pace, spreads Curse and prepares the swarm. Beast form temporarily replaces that plan with its own destructive cards.
[*][b]Lahmu swarm:[/b] raise synchronized minions that bite at the end of your turn and return protection at the start of the enemy turn.
[*][b]Curse economy:[/b] the marks Lily plants feed Lahmu damage and become a resource consumed by the Beast's strongest effects.
[*][b]Nammu Dur-an-ki:[/b] activate at 100 NP for frequent one-turn Beast windows, or bank up to 300 for three consecutive turns of devastation.
[/list]

[h2]Content[/h2]

[*]47 card models, 15 character powers and 4 relics in total
[*]Animated Tiamat-Larva and Beast identities with official FGO visuals
[*]Curated official artwork for cards, powers, relics and character UI
[*]Spanish, English, Simplified Chinese, Korean and Russian localization
[/list]

[h2]Mecánicas principales[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dos barajas:[/b] Lily controla el ritmo, distribuye Maldición y prepara el enjambre. La forma Bestia reemplaza temporalmente ese plan con sus propias cartas destructivas.
[*][b]Enjambre Laḫmu:[/b] criá esbirros sincronizados que muerden al final de tu turno y devuelven protección al comenzar el turno enemigo.
[*][b]Economía de Maldición:[/b] las marcas que planta Lily alimentan el daño de los Laḫmu y se vuelven un recurso para los efectos más fuertes de la Bestia.
[*][b]Nammu Dur-an-ki:[/b] activalo con 100 NP para ventanas frecuentes de un turno, o acumulá hasta 300 para tres turnos consecutivos de devastación.
[/list]

[list]
[*][b]双牌组：[/b]Lily 负责控制节奏、施加诅咒并准备虫群；兽形态会暂时用自己的破坏性卡牌替换这一计划。
[*][b]拉赫穆虫群：[/b]培育可同步的从属单位，它们在你的回合结束时啃咬，并在敌方回合开始时提供防护。
[*][b]诅咒经济：[/b]Lily 种下的标记会强化拉赫穆伤害，也会成为兽形态强力效果所消耗的资源。
[*][b]Nammu Dur-an-ki：[/b]在 100 NP 时发动可获得频繁的一回合兽形态；积累到 300 则可连续破坏三个回合。
