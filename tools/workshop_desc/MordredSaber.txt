[h1]Mordred — Knight of Rebellion[/h1]
Break defenses, gather Critical Stars and decide how much safety to abandon before unleashing Clarent Blood Arthur.

[b]RELEASE BUILD — v0.1.10[/b]

[h2]Core gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Masked form:[/b] the helmet keeps Mordred stable and defensive, retaining Block while she prepares the next offensive turn.
[*][b]Rebellion form:[/b] removing the helmet increases both damage dealt and damage received. Enter for explosive turns, then manage the risk you created.
[*][b]Crimson economy:[/b] trade HP for Critical Stars and turn critical hits into NP Charge. Quick attacks also feed the shared FGO Core Star system.
[*][b]Clarent Blood Arthur:[/b] the Noble Phantasm removes the helmet before dealing its hits, ensuring the entire attack receives Rebellion's offensive bonus.
[/list]

[h2]Content[/h2]
[list]
[*]68-card reward pool and a 10-card starting deck
[*]Manifested Noble Phantasm and 12 character relics
[*]Animated Masked and Rebellion forms with official FGO visuals
[*]Curated official artwork for cards, powers, relics and character UI
[*]Spanish, English, Simplified Chinese, Korean and Russian localization
[/list]

[*][b]Forma Rebelión:[/b] quitarse el casco aumenta tanto el daño causado como el recibido. Entrá para explotar y administrá después el riesgo que generaste.
[*][b]Economía carmesí:[/b] intercambiá Vida por Estrellas y convertí los críticos en Carga NP. Los Ataques Quick también alimentan el sistema global de FGO Core.

[h1]简体中文[/h1]
击碎防御、积累暴击星，并在释放 Clarent Blood Arthur 前决定愿意放弃多少安全性。

[b]公开版本 — v0.1.10[/b]

[h2]核心机制[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]戴盔形态：[/b]头盔让莫德雷德保持稳定防守，并保留格挡以准备下一次进攻回合。
[*][b]反叛形态：[/b]摘下头盔会同时提高造成与受到的伤害。抓住爆发窗口，也要承担之后的风险。
[*][b]绯红经济：[/b]用生命换取暴击星，再把暴击转化为 NP 充能；Quick 攻击同样会为 FGO Core 的共用暴击系统供能。
[*][b]Clarent Blood Arthur：[/b]宝具会在造成各段伤害前摘下头盔，使整次攻击都获得反叛形态的进攻加成。
[/list]
