[h1]Nagao Kagetora → Uesugi Kenshin[/h1]
Play a fully animated Fate/Grand Order character built around military formations, Critical Stars, Noble Phantasms and an irreversible mid-combat ascension.

[b]PLAYTEST BUILD — v0.1.11[/b]
This build is feature-complete enough for testing, but its balance and presentation are not final.

[h2]Core gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Three Precepts:[/b] advance Heaven, Chest and Feet to gain NP Charge, Block and Critical Stars. Kagetora must follow their military order; Kenshin can command them in any distinct order.
[*][b]Command Cards:[/b] combine Buster damage, Arts NP generation and Quick Critical Stars. Spend 50 Stars to empower an eligible Attack with a ×1.5 critical multiplier.
[*][b]Noble Phantasm:[/b] fill the NP gauge to manifest Biten Hassou. Kagetora's first NP completes her formation and permanently ascends her into Uesugi Kenshin.
[*][b]Two identities:[/b] both forms have their own official FGO combat model and animations. Kenshin gains a more flexible Doctrine, her own NP and anti-Man tools.
[/list]

[h2]Content[/h2]
[list]
[*]68-card reward pool and a 10-card starting deck
[*]2 manifested Noble Phantasm cards
[*]25 character powers and 12 relics, including 3 starting relics
[*]Animated Kagetora and Kenshin forms: idle, Quick attack, cast, hurt and death
[*]Optional 1024 px HD animation resources selected through FGO Core's visual-quality setting, with a safe 768 px fallback
[*]Curated official artwork for every card, power, relic and character UI element
[*]Spanish, English, Simplified Chinese, Korean and Russian localization
[*]Designed with multiplayer synchronization and shared FGO mechanics in mind
[/list]

[h1]简体中文[/h1]
以可完整游玩的动画角色体验长尾景虎，并在战斗中不可逆地升华为上杉谦信。角色玩法围绕军阵、暴击星与宝具展开。

[b]测试版本 — v0.1.11[/b]
当前版本已具备完整测试内容，但平衡与视觉表现仍非最终版本。

[h2]核心机制[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]三令制法：[/b]推进天、胸、足，分别获得 NP 充能、格挡与暴击星。景虎必须依军令顺序推进；谦信则可自由排列三个不同阶段。
[*][b]指令卡：[/b]结合 Buster 伤害、Arts NP 充能与 Quick 暴击星。消耗 50 星，使符合条件的攻击获得 ×1.5 暴击倍率。
[*][b]宝具：[/b]充满 NP 后显现「毘天八相」。景虎首次发动宝具会完成军阵，并永久升华为上杉谦信。
[*][b]双形态：[/b]两个形态均使用 FGO 官方战斗模型与动画。谦信拥有更灵活的制法、独立宝具与对「人」特攻手段。
[/list]

[h2]内容[/h2]
[list]
[*]68 张奖励卡牌与 10 张初始牌组
[*]2 张显现型宝具牌
[*]25 个角色能力与 12 件专属遗物，其中 3 件为初始遗物
[*]景虎与谦信完整动画：待机、Quick 攻击、施法、受伤与死亡
[*]每张卡牌、能力、遗物与角色界面元素均采用精选官方美术
[*]支持西班牙语、英语、简体中文、韩语与俄语
[*]按多人同步与 FGO 共用机制设计
[/list]
