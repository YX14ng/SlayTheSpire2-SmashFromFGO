Mash Kyrielight, the Shielder of Fate/Grand Order, as a playable character. Built on the BaseLib framework and the FGO Core library.

About this mod:
- Adds Mash as a fully playable, fully animated character with her own card pool and starting relic.
- A defensive playstyle built around Bulwark (block retention) and her Noble Phantasm, Lord Camelot: turn a wall of block into damage.
- In co-op, she can shield and protect her allies, not only herself.
- A custom Affinity (Bond) relic that grows stronger the more you play her.

Requires: FGO Core (library) + BaseLib — subscribe to both.
Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.

《Fate/Grand Order》的盾兵——玛修·基列莱特，作为可玩角色登场。基于 BaseLib 框架与 FGO 核心库开发。

模组介绍：
- 新增玛修为完整可玩、带完整动画的角色，拥有专属卡池与初始遗物。
- 以「壁垒」（格挡保留）与其宝具「Lord Camelot」为核心的防御流玩法：把层层格挡转化为伤害。
- 在合作模式中，她不仅能保护自己，还能为队友提供护盾与守护。
- 专属「羁绊」遗物，随你使用她的次数越多而越强。
