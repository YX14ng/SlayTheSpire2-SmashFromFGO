Mash Kyrielight, the Shielder of Fate/Grand Order - Chaldea's first Demi-Servant - as a fully playable, fully animated character. Built on the BaseLib framework and the FGO Core library.

What it adds:
- A complete character: her own card pool, starting relic, and animated battle sprite.
- A defensive engine built on Bulwark - block that carries over between turns. Stack a wall, then convert it into damage with cards like Camelot Ram.
- Her Noble Phantasm, Lord Camelot: charge your NP gauge to open a one-turn window that empowers your deck and turns defense into a decisive blow.
- Co-op support: in multiplayer, Mash can throw her shield over an ally - taking hits and granting block to the whole party, not just herself.
- A custom Affinity (Bond) relic that grows stronger the more runs you play her.

Requires: FGO Core (library) + BaseLib - subscribe to both.
Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.

「Fate/Grand Order」的盾兵——迦勒底首位准从者玛修·基列莱特，现已作为可操作、全动画的角色登场。基于 BaseLib 框架与 FGO Core 库制作。

本 Mod 带来：
- 一名完整角色：专属卡池、初始圣遗物，以及全套战斗动画立绘。
- 围绕「壁垒」(Bulwark) 构筑的防御核心——能跨回合保留的格挡。先堆出一面墙，再用「Camelot Ram」之类的卡把它转化成伤害。
- 宝具「Lord Camelot」：充满宝具槽即可开启一回合的强化窗口，强化整副牌组，把防御化作决定性的一击。
- 多人协力：联机时玛修能把盾举到队友头上，替全队挡刀并提供格挡，而不只是护住自己。
- 专属羁绊（好感度）圣遗物，随着你反复用她开局而愈发强力。

依赖：FGO Core（库）+ BaseLib——两者都要订阅。
支持语言：英文、西班牙文、简体中文。
