[h1]Gilgamesh — The Treasury Is a Weapon[/h1]
Open the Gate of Babylon, spend the run's Gold during combat and punish every enemy deemed unworthy of facing the King of Heroes.

[b]RELEASE BUILD — v0.1.11[/b]

[h2]Core gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Gold in combat:[/b] Gilgamesh is built around spending the same Gold used at shops. Wealth becomes immediate power, but every extravagant turn changes the rest of the run.
[*][b]Gate of Babylon:[/b] generate eight distinct 0-cost Treasure Arms with attacks, defense, control and setup instead of one repeated token.
[*][b]Four build paths:[/b] Arsenal, Judgement, Extravagance and Avarice support different relationships with generated weapons, Gold and the King's Arrogance.
[*][b]Enuma Elish:[/b] consume Treasure Arms from the hand and scale the anti-divine climax with stored Overcharge.
[/list]

[h2]Content[/h2]
[list]
[*]43-card reward pool and a 10-card starting deck
[*]8 distinct Treasure Arms and manifested Noble Phantasm cards
[*]8 character relics
[*]Animated official FGO battle model and curated official artwork throughout the mod
[*]Spanish, English, Simplified Chinese, Korean and Russian localization
[/list]

[h2]Requirements and compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3737335127]BaseLib 3.3.6+[/url]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747876334]FGO Core 0.1.14+[/url]
[*]Built for MAIN 0.107.1 and public BETA 0.110.1
[/list]

[h2]Mecánicas principales[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Oro en combate:[/b] Gilgamesh gasta el mismo Oro que se usa en las tiendas. La riqueza se vuelve poder inmediato, pero cada turno extravagante cambia el resto de la partida.
[*][b]Gate of Babylon:[/b] genera ocho Armas del Tesoro distintas de coste 0, con ataque, defensa, control y preparación en lugar de una ficha repetida.
[*][b]Cuatro caminos:[/b] Arsenal, Juicio, Extravagancia y Avaricia proponen relaciones diferentes con las armas generadas, el Oro y la Arrogancia del Rey.
[*][b]Enuma Elish:[/b] consume las Armas del Tesoro de la mano y escala su clímax antideidad con la Sobrecarga acumulada.
[/list]

[h1]简体中文[/h1]
开启王之财宝，在战斗中消耗本局金币，并惩罚所有不配面对英雄王的敌人。

[b]公开版本 — v0.1.11[/b]

[h2]核心机制[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]战斗中消费金币：[/b]吉尔伽美什会使用商店所需的同一份金币。财富能立刻转化为力量，但每个奢华回合都会改变整局游戏的资源规划。
[*][b]Gate of Babylon：[/b]生成八种不同的零费王之财宝衍生卡，覆盖攻击、防御、控制与准备，而非重复同一种衍生物。
[*][b]四条构筑路线：[/b]武库、审判、奢华与贪婪分别围绕衍生武器、金币及王的傲慢展开。
[*][b]Enuma Elish：[/b]消耗手中的王之财宝，并以积累的过充能强化对神性高潮。
[/list]

[h2]内容[/h2]
[list]
[*]43 张奖励卡牌与 10 张初始牌组
[*]8 种不同的王之财宝与显现型宝具牌
[*]8 件角色专属遗物
[*]FGO 官方战斗模型动画及贯穿整个 Mod 的精选官方美术
[*]支持西班牙语、英语、简体中文、韩语与俄语
[/list]
