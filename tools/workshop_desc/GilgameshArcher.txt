Gilgamesh, the King of Heroes (Archer) of Fate/Grand Order, as a fully playable character. Built on the BaseLib framework and the FGO Core library.

What it adds:
- The only character who spends the run's GOLD as a combat resource, alongside the Gate of Babylon: a tribe of eight unique 0-cost Treasure Arm tokens manifested to your hand.
- Four draftable archetypes (Arsenal, Judgement, Extravagance, Avarice) plus Critical Stars and a structural "King's Arrogance" risk that rewards never deigning to defend.
- His Noble Phantasm, Enuma Elish - the base damage is fixed, but the anti-divine bonus scales with banked Overcharge and consumes the Treasure Arms in your hand.

Requires: FGO Core (library) + BaseLib.
Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.

Made with the help of Claude Code.

「Fate/Grand Order」的英雄王吉尔伽美什（弓兵），现已作为可操作角色登场。基于 BaseLib 框架与 FGO Core 库制作。

本 Mod 带来：
- 唯一一名把本局攒下的「金币」当作战斗资源来花的角色，配合「王之财宝」(Gate of Babylon)：会在你手中显化八张各不相同、零费的王之财宝（Treasure Arm）衍生卡。
- 四套可构筑的流派（武库 Arsenal、审判 Judgement、奢华 Extravagance、贪婪 Avarice），外加暴击星，以及贯穿构筑的「王的傲慢」(King's Arrogance) 风险机制——奖励那个不屑于防御的你。
- 宝具「Enuma Elish」——基础伤害固定，但对「神性」的特攻加成会随累积的超充能（Overcharge）增长，并消耗你手牌中的王之财宝。

依赖：FGO Core（库）+ BaseLib。
支持语言：英文、西班牙文、简体中文。

在 Claude Code 的协助下制作。
