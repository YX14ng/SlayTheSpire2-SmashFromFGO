[h1]Oberon — Borrow Now, Let the Lie Pay Later[/h1]
Take NP Charge and Critical Stars from a dream, delay the bill and decide who pays when the story finally collapses.

[b]RELEASE BUILD — v0.1.18[/b]

[h2]Core gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Debt:[/b] gain powerful resources immediately, then repay them with NP Charge at the end of the turn. Unpaid Debt deals damage and accumulates interest.
[*][b]Controlled insolvency:[/b] borrow for tempo, refinance through cards and choose whether the current fight or the future deck should carry the cost.
[*][b]Three faces of the lie:[/b] the Storybook King improves the first loan, the Winter Prince delays payment and Vortigern converts remaining Debt into damage against every enemy.
[*][b]Rye Rhyme Goodfellow:[/b] build NP and Overcharge toward a Noble Phantasm that makes the world answer for Oberon's unpaid promise.
[/list]

[h2]Content[/h2]
[list]
[*]68-card reward pool and a 10-card starting deck
[*]Manifested Noble Phantasm and 11 character relics
[*]Animated Oberon and Vortigern identities with official FGO visuals
[*]Curated official artwork for cards, powers, relics and character UI
[*]Spanish, English, Simplified Chinese, Korean and Russian localization
[/list]

[list]
[*][b]债务：[/b]立刻获得强力资源，并在回合结束时以 NP 充能偿还。未付债务会造成伤害并积累利息。
[*][b]可控破产：[/b]借款换取节奏、用卡牌再融资，并选择由当前战斗还是未来牌组承担成本。
[*][b]谎言的三张面孔：[/b]童话之王强化首笔借款，冬之王子延后付款，Vortigern 则将剩余债务化为对所有敌人的伤害。
[*][b]Rye Rhyme Goodfellow：[/b]积累 NP 与过充能，让宝具迫使整个世界为奥伯龙未偿还的承诺买单。
[/list]
