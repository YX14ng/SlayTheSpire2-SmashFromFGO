Oberon (Pretender) of Fate/Grand Order, as a fully playable character. Built on the BaseLib framework and the FGO Core library.

What it adds:
- The dream-lender: a Debt resource you accrue for NP and Stars NOW, paid off by your NP charge at end of turn - or it bleeds you and grows interest.
- Three forms that change the terms: the Storybook King sweetens the first loan, the Winter Prince defers the bill, and Vortigern inverts everything - unpaid Debt becomes AoE damage when the dream collapses.
- His Noble Phantasm, Rye Rhyme Goodfellow - now consumes your built-up Debt for extra damage, so the lie you sold gets collected against the world.

Requires: FGO Core (library) + BaseLib.
Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.
