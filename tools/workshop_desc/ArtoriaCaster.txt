Artoria Caster - Castoria, the Child of Prophecy - of Fate/Grand Order, as a fully playable, fully animated support character. Built on the BaseLib framework and the FGO Core library.

What it adds:
- A complete support character with her own card pool and starting relic.
- A crit-support playstyle: spin up Critical Stars, charge your Noble Phantasm, and hand defense, buffs and resources to your whole team.
- Form switching between her Avalon and Prophecy aspects, plus Anti-Purge protection that keeps your buffs from being stripped.
- Her Noble Phantasm, Around Caliburn - "the star of hope that embraces you."
- Co-op centerpiece: in multiplayer she shines as a team enabler, feeding Stars, block and NP charge to allies so the whole party hits harder.

Requires: FGO Core (library) + BaseLib.
Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.
