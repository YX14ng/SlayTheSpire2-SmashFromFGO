[h1]Artoria Caster — The Star of Hope[/h1]
Prepare the battlefield as Fate/Grand Order's Child of Prophecy, then cross into a summer Berserker to cash stored NP and Critical Stars into a boss-killing turn.

[b]RELEASE BUILD — v0.1.20[/b]

[h2]Core gameplay[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Three forms:[/b] Caster generates NP, Stars and protection; Berserker spends that setup on aggressive critical turns; Avalon permanently combines both identities without the defensive penalty.
[*][b]Global Critical system:[/b] Quick attacks generate Critical Stars through FGO Core. At 50 Stars, an eligible Attack gains ×1.5 damage across every hit—the same rules used by the rest of the roster.
[*][b]Anti-Purge Defense:[/b] limited charges completely negate incoming enemy Attack hits, creating a powerful but timing-sensitive layer beyond Block.
[*][b]Two Noble Phantasms:[/b] crossing the NP threshold as Caster/Avalon manifests Around Caliburn; crossing it as Berserker manifests Hope Will Camelot. Your active form decides whether the climax protects or executes.
[/list]

[*]Animated Caster, Summer Berserker and Avalon forms
[*]Party support designed for co-op without losing solo value
[*]Spanish, English, Simplified Chinese, Korean and Russian localization
[/list]

[list]
[*][b]Tres formas:[/b] Caster genera NP, Estrellas y protección; Berserker gasta esa preparación en turnos críticos; Avalon combina permanentemente ambas identidades sin la penalización defensiva.
[*][b]Sistema global de Críticos:[/b] los Ataques Quick generan Estrellas mediante FGO Core. Con 50, un Ataque elegible causa ×1,5 en todos sus impactos, usando exactamente las mismas reglas que el resto del elenco.
[*][b]Anti-Purga:[/b] cargas limitadas anulan por completo impactos de Ataques enemigos y crean una capa poderosa, pero sensible al momento, más allá del Bloqueo.
[*][b]Dos Noble Phantasms:[/b] cruzar el umbral en Caster/Avalon manifiesta Around Caliburn; hacerlo en Berserker manifiesta Hope Will Camelot. La forma activa decide si el clímax protege o ejecuta.

[h1]简体中文[/h1]
先以《Fate/Grand Order》的预言之子准备战场，再切换为夏日狂战士，将积累的 NP 与暴击星化为击倒 Boss 的回合。

[b]公开版本 — v0.1.20[/b]

[h2]核心机制[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]三种形态：[/b]Caster 生成 NP、暴击星与防护；Berserker 把准备转化为进攻暴击回合；Avalon 永久结合两种身份且移除防御惩罚。
[*][b]全局暴击系统：[/b]Quick 攻击通过 FGO Core 生成暴击星。达到 50 星后，符合条件的攻击全部段数获得 ×1.5 伤害，与其他角色使用完全相同的规则。
[*][b]对肃正防御：[/b]有限层数可完全无效化敌方攻击段数，形成超越格挡、但必须谨慎把握时机的防线。
[*][b]双宝具：[/b]Caster/Avalon 形态突破 NP 阈值会显现 Around Caliburn；Berserker 则显现 Hope Will Camelot。当前形态决定高潮是守护还是处决。
[/list]

[h2]内容[/h2]
