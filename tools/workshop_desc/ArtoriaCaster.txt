Artoria Caster (Castoria) of Fate/Grand Order, as a playable character. Built on the BaseLib framework and the FGO Core library.

About this mod:
- Adds Artoria Caster as a fully playable, fully animated support character.
- A crit-support playstyle: generate Critical Stars, charge NP, and hand defense and buffs to your whole party.
- In co-op she shines as a team enabler, feeding Stars, block and NP charge to allies.

Requires: FGO Core (library) + BaseLib.
Localization: English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese.

《Fate/Grand Order》的 Caster 阿尔托莉雅（卡斯托莉雅），作为可玩角色登场。基于 BaseLib 框架与 FGO 核心库开发。

模组介绍：
- 新增 Caster 阿尔托莉雅为完整可玩、带完整动画的辅助角色。
- 以「暴击辅助」为核心的玩法：产出暴击星、积攒宝具充能，并为全队提供防御与增益。
- 在合作模式中，她是出色的队伍核心：为队友供给暴击星、格挡与宝具充能。

前置：FGO 核心库 + BaseLib。
本地化支持：英语、西班牙语、简体中文。
